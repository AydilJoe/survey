```
v1.7 — this is what one week of polish looks like.

We shipped the multi-currency + budget pools + carryover features in v1.6 last week. Then real users stress-tested it on real phones, and surfaced a stack of friction points. Some were small (the "Late night" greeting was reading slightly accusatory at 1am). Some were genuine bugs (the bulk-pay dialog had no way to edit amounts — paying extra to attack principal needed an entire workaround).

Nine of those went out today.

🥧 SPENDING PIE CHART
Top 6 expense categories on Reports. Hover for amount + %. Pure SVG, no library.

↗ QUICK-PAY ON DEBTS
Every debt row now has a Pay button. Click → Home tab opens with that debt pre-selected and the minimum pre-filled. Type the amount. Submit. Used to take 5+ taps; now it's 2.

✏️ EDITABLE BULK-PAY
The bulk-pay dialog used to lock you to the minimum payment per debt. Want to attack principal on the highest-APR card? You couldn't, in-dialog. Now every amount is editable, total updates live, and the confirm clamps to the current balance so a typo can't go negative.

👀 DEBT-AT-A-GLANCE
A total-debt summary now sits inside the home hero card. Total balance + count + "debt-free by Aug 2034" date from the avalanche simulator. Tap → Debts tab.

📱 MOBILE LAYOUT REWRITE
2×2 stat tiles instead of 4-in-a-row that truncated to "RECURRI..." on phones. 7 tabs compress to icon-only so all fit without horizontal scroll. Daily entry rows stack the FX badge below the amount instead of overlapping.

🌏 CURRENCY UPGRADE
Switched the FX upstream from Frankfurter (17 currencies, ECB-backed) to fawazahmed0/currency-api (40+ currencies, public domain). The AED/SAR/VND "display only" constraint is gone. Manual refresh in Settings.

🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST UNCHANGED
Everything still encrypted on-device with AES-GCM (PBKDF2 derived key from your passcode). Nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly tap "Sync to Drive". Receipt OCR runs locally via Tesseract.js.

Free to start.
Pay-once Pro: RM 19.90 lifetime. Receipt OCR + multi-currency + unlimited debts/goals + reminders. No subscription. Ever.
```
