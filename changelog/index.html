What's new in Duitful

Duitful ships as often as it needs to. Every change listed below lands via Vercel on duitful.app — installed PWAs auto-reload on the next open.

July 2026 · v1.11

The return your bank statement won't tell you

A holding that "went up RM 2,100" tells you almost nothing if you kept topping it up along the way. Duitful now computes the number that accounts for that: your money-weighted return — and refuses to guess when there isn't enough history to say it honestly.

  • newMoney-weighted return — per holding and for the whole portfolio, annualised since inception. It weighs every top-up, withdrawal and cash dividend at the date it actually happened against today's value, solved numerically rather than approximated. Reinvested dividends are correctly not counted as new money.
  • newPortfolio value over time — a chart in Reports built from every valuation you've recorded, each holding carried forward to every snapshot date. Hand-drawn SVG, no chart library, nothing fetched.
  • newYield on cost + per-account totals — trailing 12-month dividends against what you actually contributed (a truer figure than yield on current value), and your portfolio grouped by account once you hold more than one.
  • noteHonesty rails — under 90 days of history, or when the maths genuinely has no answer (a wiped-out holding), you get "—", never an annualised guess or a clamped endpoint.
July 2026 · v1.10.1

Unlock with your fingerprint

Typing a passcode twenty times a day is how people end up choosing "1111". In the Android app you can now unlock with a fingerprint or your face — without changing what actually protects your data.

  • newBiometric unlock, opt-in — Settings → Security → "Unlock with fingerprint / Face ID". Your passcode is kept in the phone's hardware keystore and released only after the OS scan succeeds. The passcode stays the only encryption key; biometrics just fetch it for you.
  • noteFails safe — if your phone's fingerprints or faces change, the stored passcode is invalidated and the feature switches itself off. Passcode entry is always there, and changing your passcode re-stores the new one seamlessly. Web app unchanged — this is native-only.
July 2026 · v1.10

Investments — holdings, dividends and net worth

Duitful now tracks what you own, not just what you owe. ASB, EPF, Tabung Haji, unit trusts, shares — typed in from your statements, because fetching live prices would tell a server exactly what you hold. Manual by design, private by consequence.

  • newHoldings on the Savings tab — balance-style accounts (ASB, EPF, FD) or units × price (unit trusts, shares). Top up, withdraw, revalue, and log dividends as cash or reinvested — each recorded distinctly, so the coming performance update can compute honest returns.
  • newNet worth, finally — savings + investments − debts, on the dashboard. Plus a 12-month dividend total and yield once you log dividends.
  • newZakat-aware, invisibly — if you track zakat, zakatable holdings feed your base automatically (EPF sits out by default, assessed on withdrawal). If you don't, the investments card shows nothing zakat-related at all.
  • noteFree tier: 2 holdings — unlimited on Pro. Valuations and dividends are unlimited for everyone. Everything round-trips through CSV backup.

July 2026 · v1.9

Islamic financing for everyone — no mode to switch on

The Shariah-mode toggle is gone. Islamic financing is a product type, not an identity setting — plenty of Malaysian borrowers hold an AITAB car facility or Tawarruq personal financing regardless of faith — so the Islamic debt type now sits alongside Standard and Installment for every user. Zakat becomes a quiet opt-in where it belongs, on the Savings tab.

  • newIslamic debt type, always available — pick it in Debts like any other type. Same fixed-profit maths, ibra' estimate and effective-rate ranking as v1.8; there is simply nothing to enable first.
  • newZakat is one tap on the Savings tab — “Set up zakat tracking” adds the card, and the nisab & haul settings now live on the card itself instead of a separate Settings section. Stop tracking any time; your numbers are kept.
  • noteThe Settings → Islamic finance card is gone — it only gated these two things. Existing zakat setups carry over untouched, and per-contract labelling is unchanged: Islamic rows say profit rate, conventional rows say APR.
July 2026 · v1.8.1

Every debt speaks its own contract

v1.8 swapped the whole app's vocabulary when you turned Shariah mode on, which meant a conventional credit card sitting next to a Tawarruq facility got labelled “Rate 18.00%” instead of “APR 18.00%”. That card charges interest, and softening the word to make one list read consistently misdescribed the contract you actually signed. Labels are now chosen per debt.

  • fixConventional debts keep saying APR — even with Shariah mode on, and even in the same list as an Islamic facility. Your credit card row reads “APR 18.00%”, your Tawarruq reads “Profit rate 9.44% eff.”, and the payoff queue shows both side by side. The add-debt form and edit dialog follow the same rule.
  • newPortfolio totals blend when you hold both — “Total interest” if all your debts are conventional, “Total profit charged” if all are Islamic, and “Total interest + profit” with a “weighted rate” when you have a mix. Decided by what you actually hold, not by the toggle — so if Shariah mode is on but your only debt is a credit card, Duitful says interest, because that's what you're paying.
  • noteThe toggle does less than it used to — it unlocks the Islamic debt type and the zakat tracker. It no longer changes wording for debts that aren't Islamic. Existing Islamic debts stay fully editable with the mode switched off; only the option to add a new one is hidden.
  • fixDocs corrected — the v1.8 notes, guides and machine-readable summary all described the old app-wide relabelling. Updated to match how it actually behaves.
July 2026 · v1.8

Shariah mode — Islamic financing and zakat, free for everyone

Duitful now speaks Islamic finance properly. Track Murabahah, Tawarruq, BBA, AITAB, Ijarah and Musharakah Mutanaqisah facilities with the maths they actually use — fixed profit that never compounds, and an ibra' estimate showing what settling early really saves. Plus a full zakat-on-wealth tracker with nisab and haul. Both are free on every tier, not Pro features.

  • newShariah mode (free) — Settings → Islamic finance. Relabels the app for Islamic contracts: “profit rate” instead of APR, “profit charges” instead of interest, throughout. Toggle it any time; nothing needs a reload, and turning it off leaves your data untouched.
  • newIslamic financing debt type (free) — a new debt kind alongside Standard and Installment, covering Murabahah, Tawarruq, BBA, AITAB, Ijarah and Musharakah Mutanaqisah. Enter the financed amount, total profit and tenure straight off your offer letter. Duitful shows the instalment, the total payable, and the effective rate live as you type — before you sign anything.
  • newIbra' on early settlement — your bank statement shows the outstanding sale price, which includes profit you haven't been charged yet. Duitful shows the outstanding principal — what settling today actually costs — with the ibra' spelled out beside it, so the two numbers reconcile. The payoff simulator models profit that stops accruing once the principal clears, so accelerating a facility finally shows up as real savings instead of no change at all.
  • newPayoff queue understands flat rates — an Islamic facility has no APR, but it isn't free either. Duitful converts the flat profit to an effective annual rate, so a 4.8% flat facility queues at roughly 9.44% — correctly ahead of an 8% card and behind a 15% one. Previously the avalanche planner would have sorted it last on a 0% APR and left you clearing the wrong debt for years.
  • newZakat on wealth (free) — a zakat card on the Savings tab. Nisab from 85 g gold, 595 g silver, or your state authority's published figure. Your zakatable base builds from savings-goal balances plus wealth you enter manually (ASB, unit trusts, cash, gold), less immediate debts. 2.5% is levied on the whole base once nisab is met — not on the excess, which is the mistake that understates most people's zakat by roughly two-thirds.
  • newHaul countdown — set the date you crossed nisab and Duitful counts down the 354-day lunar year, so the date stops being a surprise. Marking zakat paid records the payment, resets the haul from that date, and logs the amount as a real expense — it belongs in your cash flow, not in a footnote.
  • noteZakat figures are estimates, not rulings — rules vary by state and by asset type, and ibra' is modelled as a full straight-line rebate of unearned profit. Your bank's contract may use a less generous formula, and lock-in fees sit outside it entirely. Confirm zakat with your state authority and settlement figures with your bank.
  • newThree new guides — zakat on wealth, ibra' and early settlement, and flat rate vs effective rate, with Bahasa Melayu translations of the first two.
  • fixCSV round-trips everything — Islamic financing rows and Shariah preferences survive export and re-import. Older builds skip the columns they don't recognise, and existing CSVs import unchanged.
  • fixPayoff projection rounding — long-tenure facilities were picking up an extra month of profit because the stored instalment is rounded to sen and the sub-cent drift left a few sen outstanding at term. The projection now uses the exact instalment and caps total profit at the contracted amount.
  • fixGuide comparison blocks — two-column comparison blocks broke their layout when an item contained bold or italic text, splitting it into a separate column. Fixed across every guide.
May 2026 · v1.7

Visual reports, searchable lists, and global FX coverage

A polish-heavy follow-up to v1.6. Reports tab gets a real spending pie. Every list gets a search bar. Multi-currency now covers 46 currencies (up from 17), including AED, SAR, and VND that were display-only before. Plus a wave of UX cleanup across the app.

  • newSpending pie chart on Reports — the by-category list on Reports is now a real SVG pie with a legend, total line, and "Other" grouping for the long tail. Filters by kind (income / expense), respects your privacy toggle, and sizes up properly on desktop.
  • newSearch across all lists — a search bar on Monthly (income + expense), Daily, Debts, Savings, and the Budget Pools manager. Filters by name as you type. Auto-hides on Savings when there are too few goals to justify it.
  • newFX coverage expands to 46 currencies — switched the live-rates source from Frankfurter to the open-source Currency-API. AED, SAR, and VND now have real rates instead of being display-only. Added 26 more including ASEAN neighbours (BND, LAK, KHR, MMK, TWD), South Asia (PKR, BDT, LKR, NPR), GCC (QAR, KWD, OMR, BHD), and assorted Europe / Americas (SEK, NOK, DKK, PLN, BRL, MXN, ZAR, etc.). Server-side 24h cache with a CDN fallback.
  • fixDashboard double-count — debt payments and savings contributions were being subtracted twice from "Balance Left" in some configurations. The formula now uses max(min + extra, paid) as the floor.
  • newBudget Pools quick-start templates — first-time pool creation offers preset templates instead of a blank form so you don't have to invent categories from scratch.
  • newHonest over-budget % + dated payoff ETA — the over-budget badge shows a real percentage instead of capping at 100%. Debt payoff projection now shows the actual target month instead of "X months from now."
  • fixSettings clarity — clearer labels on every preference, Share via Web Share where supported (falls back to copy-link), neutral greeting that doesn't assume time of day.
  • fixDebts disambiguation — duplicate debt names now get a suffix so you can tell two "Maybank Card" entries apart. Due-soon legend added to the dashboard banner.
  • fixDaily pill colours — daily-entry pill backgrounds match the brand palette consistently, with an inline legend so Spend / Pay debt / Save buckets are obvious at a glance.
  • fixSavings empty state — friendlier copy when you haven't added any goals; the search bar hides itself when the list is sparse.
  • fixCross-app polish — global accent-color on form controls; dash placeholders replaced with proper empty states.
  • fixDestructive actions stand out — Delete and other destructive buttons are visually distinct from primary actions so accidental clicks are less likely.
May 2026 · v1.6

Multi-currency, budget pools, running balance, and auto-recurring

A four-feature drop. Track money in any of 20 currencies with daily-refreshed mid-market rates. Split your monthly money into named budget pools with rollover and overrides. See a true running balance that chains every previous month into today's number. And monthly income/expenses now repeat themselves so you stop retyping rent every month.

  • newMulti-currency entry (Pro) — log a USD subscription, an SGD lunch, or a JPY hotel and Duitful converts to your base currency at entry time using daily mid-market rates. The original currency + sticky exchange rate are stored on each entry, so the badge stays honest forever even if rates move. Powered by the open-source Currency-API by @fawazahmed0 — 40+ currencies, no API key, daily updates. Manual refresh in Settings. (Originally shipped with Frankfurter/ECB covering 17 currencies; switched mid-v1.6 to expand coverage and remove the AED/SAR/VND display-only constraint.)
  • newBudget pools (Pro beyond 1) — split your money into named buckets (Shopping RM 500, Bali RM 3000, Subs RM 200). Tag daily and recurring expenses to a pool to track usage. Free tier gets 1 pool plus the auto-managed Debt pool. Pro unlocks unlimited pools, single-active toggle, "Copy overrides from last month", auto-suggest by category match.
  • newPool rollover & per-month overrides (Pro) — opt-in rollover carries unspent budget into next month (chains all the way back, no artificial cap). Per-month overrides let you set a higher Shopping budget in December without changing the base limit. Both surface in the manager card under Income on the Monthly tab.
  • newAuto-managed "Debt" pool (free) — a system pool whose limit auto-derives from your debts' monthly minimums. Pay-debt entries auto-tag to it. Banner escalates from calm → yellow → red as due dates approach and pass. "Pay monthly debts" button on Home opens a bulk-pay dialog with smart-default checkboxes (already-paid debts auto-skip, partials show remaining), per-row date overrides, and one-click confirm.
  • newRunning balance carryover (free) — last month's ending balance now flows into this month's "Balance Left" stat. The chain extends all the way back to your first logged entry — January's surplus survives into March, terminating only at the earliest data month. Inline ✎ to manually correct historical debt minimums. The formula treats minimums as max(snapshot, actual paid) so skipped payments still show as a deficit you need to balance back.
  • newRecurring auto-copy (Pro) — open the app on the 1st of a new month and your salary, rent, subscriptions are already there. Per-entry "Repeat next month" checkbox (defaults on) on the income and expense forms — untick the toggle for one-off entries (year-end bonus, wedding-month rent) so they don't recur. Dedupes by name and amount, preserves currency conversions and pool tags on the copies.
  • fixBillplz checkout — payment was failing with a 401 from the Billplz API. Production environment variables aligned to the live key set; new bills now flow through cleanly.
  • fixFX picker layout — the currency dropdown next to the amount field no longer squeezes the input on narrow screens; symbol shown inline (e.g. "JPY (¥)", "MYR (RM)", "USD ($)") for faster recognition.
  • fixBulk-pay debt dialog row layout — date input now stacks on its own line at narrow widths, fixing the meta-text wrap that made rows look cramped.
  • noteStorage growth — none of these features add per-day data. The new state slots (per-month debt minimum snapshots, last-opened-month pointer, budget pool definitions) total under 2 KB even after a decade of monthly use.

April 2026 · v1.4

Multi-device sync via your Google Drive

Pro users can now sync their tracker across devices using their own Google Drive. Duitful runs no sync server. The encrypted database file is written to your Drive; other devices sign in with the same Google account, pull the file, and decrypt it locally with your passcode.

  • newEncrypted Drive sync (Pro) — your database is encrypted with your passcode before it leaves the device. The blob in Drive is unreadable to anyone, including Google and including me.
  • newRestore on a new device — install Duitful on iPhone or laptop, sign in with the same Google, paste the passcode, and your data is back.
  • newNo new account on Duitful — Google sign-in is just for Drive access; you still don't have a Duitful account.
