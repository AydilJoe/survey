## The two dates that matter

:::stat
value: June 1, 2026
label: Mandatory affordability checks + fee disclosure for all BNPL providers
note: CCA itself is already in force since March 1, 2026
:::

The Consumer Credit Act regulates BNPL providers (Atome, SPayLater, Grab PayLater, ShopBack PayLater, and others) under the new Consumer Credit Commission. The framework is in force now. The strict consumer-side rules kick in on June 1.

## What changes for you

:::steps
title: Affordability check before every new BNPL plan
text: Providers must verify that you can actually pay before approving a new instalment. Expect more "no" replies if you have thin track records or high existing debt across providers.
---
title: All fees disclosed before you confirm
text: Late fees, conversion fees, missed-instalment penalties — every charge has to be on screen before you tap **Confirm**. No discovery after the fact.
---
title: A single regulator to escalate to
text: If a BNPL provider misbehaves, the Consumer Credit Commission is the place to file a complaint. Provider-side complaint channels still apply first, but there's now a real backstop.
:::

## Before CCA vs after

:::compare
title: Before CCA
- Approvals on minimal checks across providers
- Fees discovered after a missed payment
- Disputes handled provider by provider
- No central oversight
---
title: After June 1, 2026
- Affordability check before approval
- All fees on screen at confirm
- One regulator (Consumer Credit Commission)
- Stricter rules but cleaner consumer protection
:::

## Tracking BNPL cleanly in Duitful

:::steps
title: Add each plan as a debt
text: Open **Debts → Add**. Enter the provider name (Atome, SPayLater), the outstanding balance, the APR (yes, BNPL has one once you miss a payment), and the minimum monthly amount.
---
title: Categorise the daily deductions
text: When a payment comes off your card or wallet, log it as a daily expense. Set Category to the BNPL provider so Reports filters cleanly.
---
title: Watch the running total
text: Open the **Debts** tab. You'll see total BNPL outstanding alongside credit cards and loans on one screen. Avalanche planner sorts by APR so you know which to clear first.
:::

## Common questions

:::faq
q: Does CCA cap how much I can borrow on BNPL?
a: Not directly. The act requires providers to check affordability before approving new credit. In practice that means stricter approval for users with thin track records or high existing debt across providers.
---
q: Will my existing BNPL plans be cancelled or repriced?
a: No. Existing plans run to completion on the terms they were issued. The new rules apply to plans issued from June 1, 2026 onwards.
---
q: Where do I complain about a BNPL provider after June 1?
a: Escalate to the Consumer Credit Commission once the provider's own complaint channel has failed. CCC publishes its escalation process; check their site closer to the date.
---
q: How do I track my BNPL APR if the provider doesn't show one?
a: When you sign up, look at the late-payment fee structure. A missed instalment that triggers a 5%-of-outstanding fee is effectively very high APR. Log a conservative APR in Duitful (e.g., 24%) so the avalanche planner gives the BNPL its weight.
:::
