## The setup, in three steps

:::steps
title: Use "Labour Day 2026" as your category
text: When you log a daily expense, type `Labour Day 2026` in the **Category** field. After the first time, it's a one-tap selection from the dropdown.
---
title: Set a hard cap in your head
text: Pick the number you can afford to spend across Friday–Sunday. Petrol, makan, hotel, theme-park entry, gifts. Write it on the back of a receipt if you have to.
---
title: Categorise every long-weekend ringgit
text: Toll, fuel, mamak, AirAsia change-fee, Klang bak kut teh — they all go in the same category. Open **Reports** on Sunday night to see how close you are.
:::

## Five quick wins

:::steps
title: Set a daily mini-cap
text: If your weekend cap is RM 900, that's roughly RM 300/day. Hitting RM 280 by lunch on Saturday is your warning shot.
---
title: Pre-log the inevitable
text: Toll. Fuel. One hotel night. Log those *now* with rough estimates so the "discretionary" pool is what's actually left.
---
title: Snap restaurant bills
text: **Pro** receipt OCR pulls totals from photos. Useful when you're splitting bills and don't want to type while everyone's waiting.
---
title: Auto-FX for cross-border trips
text: JB to Singapore, KL to Krabi — log entries in SGD or THB, Duitful converts to MYR automatically and stores both.
---
title: One-screen review on Sunday
text: Open **Reports**, set Category to **Labour Day 2026**, look at the total. If you've blown past the cap, Monday becomes a no-spend day on purpose.
:::

## Common questions

:::faq
q: Why a category instead of a whole new budget?
a: Categories are zero-setup — they're built into every entry. Budgets-within-budgets get abandoned. A "Labour Day 2026" category survives the weekend and stays searchable next year.
---
q: Do I need Pro for this?
a: No. The free tier handles categories, totals, and filtering in Reports. Pro adds receipt OCR — useful, not required.
---
q: What about the Wesak / Hari Raya / Merdeka long weekends later?
a: Same trick — pick a new category name for each one (e.g., `Hari Raya 2026`). Compare year-over-year totals in Reports once you've got two or three holidays logged.
---
q: My long weekend involves Singapore. Help?
a: Log entries in SGD; Duitful converts to MYR with the day's rate and keeps both numbers. In Reports, the totals are already in MYR.
:::
