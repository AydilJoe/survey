## The setup, in three steps

:::steps
title: Make a "Labour Day" tag
text: In Duitful, **Settings → Tags → New** → call it `Labour Day 2026`. Add a colour you'll spot in your feed.
---
title: Set a hard cap in your head
text: Pick the number you can afford to spend across Friday–Sunday. Petrol, makan, hotel, theme-park entry, gifts. Write it on the back of a receipt if you have to.
---
title: Tag every long-weekend ringgit
text: Toll, fuel, mamak, AirAsia change-fee, Klang bak kut teh — they all get the same tag. Filter on Sunday night to see how close you are.
:::

## Five quick wins

:::steps
title: Set a daily mini-cap
text: If your weekend cap is RM 900, that's roughly RM 300/day. Hitting RM 280 by lunch on Saturday is your warning shot.
---
title: Pre-tag the inevitable
text: Toll. Fuel. One hotel night. Tag those *now* with rough estimates so the "discretionary" pool is what's actually left.
---
title: Snap restaurant bills
text: **Pro** receipt OCR pulls totals from photos. Useful when you're splitting bills and don't want to type while everyone's waiting.
---
title: Auto-FX for cross-border trips
text: JB to Singapore, KL to Krabi — log entries in SGD or THB, Duitful converts to MYR automatically and stores both.
---
title: One-screen review on Sunday
text: Filter by tag, look at the total. If you've blown past the cap, Monday becomes a no-spend day on purpose.
:::

## Common questions

:::faq
q: Why a tag instead of a whole new budget?
a: Tags are zero-setup — you already have the system. Budgets-within-budgets get abandoned. A single "Labour Day 2026" tag survives the weekend and stays searchable next year.
---
q: Do I need Pro for this?
a: No. The free tier handles tags, totals, and filtering. Pro adds receipt OCR and unlimited tags — useful, not required.
---
q: What about the Wesak / Hari Raya / Merdeka long weekends later?
a: Same trick — make a new tag for each one. Compare year-over-year totals once you've got two or three holidays logged.
---
q: My long weekend involves Singapore. Help?
a: Log entries in SGD; Duitful converts to MYR with the day's rate and keeps both numbers. Filter by tag to see total spend in MYR.
:::
