## The setup, in three steps

:::steps
title: Use "Petrol" as your category
text: When you log a daily expense, type **Petrol** in the **Category** field (and **Diesel** if you drive both). Next time, the dropdown auto-suggests it — one tap.
---
title: Log every fill-up
text: After each pump, add a daily expense → enter the ringgit amount → pick **Petrol** as the category → save. Ten seconds, including the receipt photo if you want OCR.
---
title: Read the monthly total
text: Open the **Reports** tab → set Category to **Petrol** → pick **This month**. The big "Total" at the top is exactly what you spent on petrol. No spreadsheet maths.
:::

## Why this matters now

:::stat
value: RM 200
label: Monthly diesel subsidy cap (Budi Madani, eligible vehicles)
note: Refer to KPDN for current eligibility & quota
:::

Budi95 is Malaysia's targeted fuel subsidy programme. Eligible drivers get subsidised RON95 or diesel up to a monthly cap; everything beyond it is at unsubsidised market rates. Without tracking, you only find out on the pump screen.

## Bonus moves

:::steps
title: Snap, don't type
text: **Pro** receipt OCR pulls the amount, date, and station name from a photo. Useful when a queue forms behind you.
---
title: Split the company car
text: Use a separate category like `Petrol · work` for fill-ups you'll claim back. Reports lets you filter by either at year-end.
---
title: Watch the trendline
text: Open **Reports**, pick **Last 3 months**, set Category to **Petrol** — the trend chart shows the curve. A 25% jump usually means a longer commute, not a price hike.
:::

## Common questions

:::faq
q: Does Duitful know my Budi95 quota balance?
a: No — only KPDN's app does. Duitful tracks what *you* spent. Compare it against your remaining quota in the official channel; Duitful tells you the ringgit half of the equation.
---
q: Can I track diesel and RON95 separately?
a: Yes. Use two categories: `RON95` and `Diesel`. Each fill-up gets one. Reports filters cleanly by category.
---
q: What if I pay with TNG e-Wallet or a fleet card?
a: Doesn't matter — log the ringgit amount, set Category to Petrol. Add the payment method to the **note** field if you want to remember which wallet it came from.
---
q: Does fuel data leave my phone?
a: No. Every entry is encrypted on-device with AES-GCM. No server has it. Even Pro receipt OCR runs locally.
:::
