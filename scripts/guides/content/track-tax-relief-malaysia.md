## What's at stake

:::stat
value: RM 34,500
label: Combined YA 2026 limits of eight reliefs most salaried people can use — lifestyle, sports, medical, two insurance reliefs, EPF, PRS and SSPN
note: Every RM 1,000 claimed saves RM 60–RM 250 for most salaried incomes, depending on your tax band.
:::

Unless you file a TP1 with your employer, PCB only counts the basic reliefs. Every other relief you can prove pushes your real tax below what was taken — and the difference comes back as a refund.

## How Duitful catches it

:::steps
title: Turn on the tax card
text: Open **Reports → Tax refund estimate → Set it up**. Type the PCB from your payslips and your gross salary once. Duitful reviews this year's spending straight away.
---
title: Log spending as usual
text: Buy a book, renew the gym, pay the dentist. When an expense matches an LHDN relief, Duitful asks: **use this for your YA 2026 claim?** Tap Yes, Later, or Not tax.
---
title: Keep the receipt — or add it later
text: After Yes, snap the receipt or attach the doctor's letter. It's optional: Duitful tells you the date to add it by (the e-Filing deadline) and what LHDN may ask for.
---
title: File with the totals ready
text: Your refund estimate updates with every claim. In March, **Export claims** gives you each relief's total to type into e-Filing.
:::

## Your receipts stay yours

Receipt photos and PDFs are encrypted with your passcode and kept on your phone — never uploaded, never in a backup file, readable only after you unlock. LHDN can ask for records for **7 years**; Duitful keeps them until you delete the claim.

## Common questions

:::faq
q: Does Duitful know this year's relief limits?
a: Yes. The YA 2026 and YA 2027 schedule is built in — including sub-limits like RM 1,000 for dental inside the RM 10,000 medical relief, and RM 300 for sports shoes from YA 2027. It's still an estimate: check LHDN's figures before you file.
---
q: How is the refund worked out?
a: Your gross income minus automatic and claimed reliefs gives chargeable income. Duitful applies the resident tax bands and the RM 400 rebate, takes off any zakat you've logged, and compares the result with the PCB you've paid.
---
q: Can one purchase count for two reliefs?
a: No. Each claim goes to one relief. Duitful suggests the best match and shows how much room is left in each, so you can pick the one you haven't filled yet.
---
q: Does any of this go to LHDN?
a: No. Duitful never sees your data. You type the totals into e-Filing yourself, and the receipts stay on your phone until LHDN asks.
:::
