## The setup, in three steps

:::steps
title: Add five tax-relief tags
text: In Duitful, **Settings → Tags → New**. Add `Medical`, `Lifestyle`, `Books`, `SSPN`, `PRS`. Five seconds each. (Add more if you have specific reliefs.)
---
title: Tag spending as it happens
text: When you log an expense, pick the matching relief tag. A doctor's bill → Medical. A new laptop under RM 2,500 → Lifestyle. A unit-trust top-up to your child's SSPN → SSPN.
---
title: Filter when March comes
text: Open **Reports**, filter by tag, get the total per relief category. Type the numbers straight into your e-Filing form. Done.
:::

## Tag ideas to copy

:::steps
title: Medical (own & parents)
text: Doctor visits, hospital bills, dental, full medical check-up. Two tags if you want to split your own vs your parents' — LHDN treats them as separate reliefs.
---
title: Lifestyle (RM 2,500 cap)
text: Books, magazines, sports gear, gym fees, internet bills, smartphone, tablet, laptop. One bucket — but tag everything that fits, then sort at year-end.
---
title: SSPN-i & PRS
text: Skim Simpan Pendidikan Nasional contributions and Private Retirement Scheme top-ups. Both have separate reliefs. Tag the moment the deposit clears.
---
title: EPF voluntary & insurance
text: Voluntary KWSP top-ups, takaful and life-insurance premiums. Easy to forget if billed annually — tagging makes them findable.
:::

## Common questions

:::faq
q: Does Duitful know LHDN's current relief amounts?
a: Numbers change yearly, so no — Duitful gives you *what you spent*, not *how much you can claim*. Cross-reference with LHDN's current relief schedule when you file.
---
q: Can I tag one expense with two reliefs?
a: Yes. A laptop under RM 2,500 might be Lifestyle and Work in your head — Duitful supports multiple tags per entry. Use both.
---
q: What about my freelance income?
a: Use a tag like `Income · freelance`. At year-end, total income and total deductible expenses are both one filter away. See the [freelancer guide](/guides/sme-freelancer-expense-tracker/) for the full setup.
---
q: Will my financial data go to LHDN through Duitful?
a: No. Duitful never sees your data — it stays encrypted on your phone. You manually type the totals into LHDN's e-Filing site, same as before.
:::
