## The maths nobody shows you

:::stat
value: RM 277,653
label: The coast number behind a RM 1.2 million target
note: A 30-year-old wanting RM 4,000/month at 60, at a 5% real return: RM 1.2 million ÷ 1.05³⁰. Reach RM 277,653 and compounding finishes the job with zero further contributions.
:::

The usual retirement conversation starts with a terrifying pot — RM 4,000 a month on the 4% rule means RM 1.2 million — and ends with you closing the tab. Coast FIRE reframes it: you don't need the whole pot. You need the seed that *becomes* the pot by retirement age.

Coast number = target ÷ (1 + real return)^years left. Every year you start earlier, the seed shrinks. That's the entire trick.

## What "real return" means in Malaysia

The rate in that formula must be a **real** return — after inflation — or you're planning in ringgit that won't exist. EPF has averaged around 5.5–6.3% in recent years and ASB in a similar band; knock off ~2–3% inflation and an honest planning range is **2–4% real**. Duitful defaults to 4% and lets you set anything from −10% to +20%, because the assumption is yours to own.

:::compare
title: Coasting ✓
- Your pot ≥ your coast number
- Future contributions are optional — they buy comfort, not survival
- A career break, a pay cut, a business attempt stops being a retirement emergency
---
title: RM X to go
- Your pot < your coast number
- Every RM 1,000 saved now is worth (1+r)^years at retirement
- The gap shown is today's money, not an inflated future figure
:::

## The honest caveats

The 4% rule is a rule of thumb from US market history, not a guarantee. Markets don't return a straight line, and EPF's dividend is declared yearly, not promised. Duitful labels every figure an estimate in today's money — treat "Coasting ✓" as permission to breathe, not to stop paying attention.

:::faq
q: Does EPF count towards my coast number?
a: Yes — it's usually the biggest slice. Add it as a holding (it defaults to non-zakatable, since zakat on EPF is typically assessed on withdrawal) and update the balance when your annual statement lands. If part of it is committed elsewhere, track only the portion you'd actually retire on.
---
q: Is RM 4,000 a month a sensible target?
a: It's a common middle-class Malaysian figure, but yours is yours: look at this month's spending in Duitful, subtract what disappears by then (instalments, children's costs), add what appears (health cover). The card recalculates instantly when you change it.
---
q: What if I'm past 45 — is coast FIRE pointless?
a: The lever is shorter but the maths still works: 15 years at 4% real still cuts the required seed by 45%. And the projection line answers the more useful question — where your *current* saving rate actually lands you, so you can adjust while adjusting still helps.
