# Claude Motion prompt — Duitful promo

Paste this into the Motion tab, then attach the screenshots in this folder (01 → 08, in order) and `duitful-icon.svg`.

---

Make a 20-second vertical (9:16, 1080×1920) launch video for **Duitful**, a privacy-first money, debt and savings tracker for Malaysia. Tone: polished, calm, modern — not hype. Hard cuts on the beat, soft eases inside scenes. Use the attached real app screenshots inside a phone frame; do not redraw the UI.

**Brand:** background cream #efe9dd, ink #241f1a, terracotta accent #c8704b, sage #6f9457. Headlines in an elegant serif (Fraunces), small text in Inter. Amounts are Malaysian ringgit (RM).

**Storyboard**
1. 0–2s — Big serif text, words appearing one by one: "Where did / your salary / go?" ("go?" in terracotta italic).
2. 2–4s — App icon pops in, wordmark "Duitful", subtitle "Private money tracker for Malaysia".
3. 4–8s — Phone slides up with screenshots 01→03. Caption: "Log a spend in seconds." Show a finger tap on the amount field, "12" typed, Add entry tapped; balance goes RM 2,248.30 → RM 2,236.30.
4. 8–12s — Screenshots 04→05. Caption: "Highest APR first. Debt-free sooner." Highlight the "Debt-free in" figure changing from 8 yr 8 mo to 4 yr 2 mo after an extra RM 300/month.
5. 12–15s — Screenshots 06→07. Caption: "Goals that fill up." Emergency Fund bar grows from 43% to 45%.
6. 15–17s — Screenshot 08. Caption: "Hide every ringgit." Amounts blur.
7. 17–20s — Phone slides away. "No account." / "No server." (second line terracotta italic), then "Encrypted on your phone. Only your passcode opens it.", then a dark pill button "duitful.app/app" and "Free to start · also on Google Play".

**Rules:** keep every line on screen long enough to read (~0.3s per word). Only use the claims above — no invented numbers or testimonials. Every frame should look good as a still. Soft, upbeat 120 BPM instrumental, cuts on the beat.
