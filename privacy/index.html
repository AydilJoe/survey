Privacy policy

Written in plain English, not legalese. The short version lives right below — the rest is the full detail you'll want if you care, or if a regulator asks.

Effective 23 April 2026 · Last updated 6 June 2026

The short version

  • Your transactions, debts, savings, receipts and notes stay on your device, encrypted with AES-GCM using a key derived from your passcode. Duitful runs no sync server.
  • If you turn on Pro's optional Google Drive sync, an already-encrypted copy of your database is uploaded directly from your device to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. Duitful never sees the bytes; Google cannot read them without your passcode.
  • Receipt scanning runs entirely on your device using Tesseract.js. Receipt images and extracted text are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • On Android, bank and e-wallet notifications are parsed on the device itself. Nothing from a notification is transmitted anywhere.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising trackers, or error-reporting SDKs. There are no tracking cookies.
  • When you buy Pro, your name and email go through Billplz (our payment processor) so we can send you a licence. That is the only time we knowingly receive personal data.
  • The Pro licence itself is a cryptographically signed token. It does not contain device identifiers and cannot be used to track you.

1. Who we are

Duitful is a personal finance application operated by an independent developer based in Malaysia ("Duitful", "we", "us", or "our"). Duitful is distributed as a progressive web app at duitful.app/app and as native apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

For any privacy question, including requests under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia) or any other applicable law, email hello@duitful.app. A human reads every message.

2. What we do not do

Because the things we don't do are central to how Duitful is built, it's easier to list them first.

  • We do not have a user account system. There is no sign-up, no login, no password stored anywhere.
  • We do not run a sync server, and we do not store your transactions in any database we operate. Pro's optional Google Drive sync writes an already-encrypted file directly from your device to your own Google Drive — the bytes never pass through any Duitful infrastructure.
  • We do not use analytics, product telemetry, crash reporting SDKs, or advertising identifiers. Not Google Analytics, Firebase, Sentry, Mixpanel, PostHog, Amplitude, Crashlytics, or anything similar.
  • We do not set tracking cookies on this website.
  • We do not sell, rent, share, or monetise personal data. There is no ad network involved anywhere in the product.
  • We do not read, scan, or upload your receipt images. OCR runs on the device and the image never leaves it.
  • We do not transmit the contents of your phone's notifications. The Android notification listener pattern-matches text locally and feeds the result into the app's own on-device database.

3. What stays on your device

All the data that makes Duitful useful to you lives in local storage on the device where you use it:

  • Income entries, expense entries, debts, savings goals, recurring items, categories, notes and settings.
  • Receipt images while they are being scanned, and the text Tesseract extracts from them.
  • Parsed bank and e-wallet notification content (Android only).
  • Foreign-exchange rate snapshots cached for up to 24 hours, and the last few UI preferences.

This data is written to the browser's localStorage (or the equivalent storage inside the native app shell). It is encrypted at rest using AES-GCM with a 256-bit key derived from your passcode via PBKDF2 (250,000 iterations, SHA-256). We do not know your passcode; we do not have a copy of the derived key; we cannot decrypt your data.

If you forget your passcode, your data is not recoverable — not even if you have a Google Drive backup, because the backup is encrypted with the same passcode. This is a deliberate trade-off for genuine end-to-end privacy. We recommend writing your passcode down somewhere safe, and using Pro's Google Drive sync or CSV export so you have a portable copy of the encrypted data itself.

Local storage keys, for transparency

Duitful writes the following keys to your browser's local storage:

  • duit-tracker.enc — the encrypted blob containing your records.
  • duit-tracker.v1 — a legacy plain-text store used only once during migration to the encrypted store, then cleared.
  • duit-tracker.referrer and duit-tracker.promo — optional referral / promo codes if you followed a link, kept for 30 days.
  • duit-tracker.fx — cached foreign-exchange rates, up to 24 hours.
  • duit-tracker.privacy — whether you have "blur balances" mode turned on.
  • duit-tracker.install-dismissed-at — the timestamp when you dismissed the "install as an app" prompt, so we don't show it again for a while.
  • duit-tracker.drive — only present if you have connected Google Drive sync (Pro). Holds the OAuth access token Google issued to your browser, the Drive file identifier of your backup, your Google account email (so the in-app status line can show which account is connected), and the timestamp of the last successful sync. Removed when you disconnect Drive sync.

4. What actually reaches us

The only situations in which personal data leaves your device and reaches us or our vendors are these:

4.1 When you buy Duitful Pro through the web checkout

Pro can be purchased through Billplz, a Malaysian payment gateway. To start a checkout, we send Billplz your email address, the name you enter at checkout, the amount (RM 19.90 or the discounted amount if a code applies), a product reference (duitful_pro), and — if you arrived via a referral link — an 8-character referral code derived from the referrer's email.

Billplz then collects your bank or card details directly on their hosted checkout page. We never see those details. When Billplz notifies us that a payment has completed, our server receives the bill identifier, the paid state, the amount, and the email you provided.

We then issue you a Pro licence (see 4.3 below) and send it to the email address you provided. Our Vercel serverless function writes a short audit record of each purchase event (bill identifier, paid state, amount, email, any discount code used) to its execution log, so we can diagnose payment issues. These logs are kept by Vercel for a limited period (generally up to 30 days) and are not used for marketing, profiling, or any other purpose.

4.2 When you buy Duitful Pro inside the native iOS or Android app

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). Duitful does not receive your payment details or see your Apple ID / Google account. Those stores operate under their own privacy policies, which you accepted when you set up your device. Purchase receipts are validated locally using each store's standard SDK.

4.3 The Pro licence token

Once payment is confirmed, we generate a Pro licence. It is a short signed token, using ECDSA on the P-256 curve. The payload contains: a subject identifier (your Billplz bill ID, or a complimentary code for free licences), the email address you provided at checkout, the product code (duitful_pro), the referral code if any, the issue timestamp, and — when applicable — the discount source. The token is verified on your device with a public key built into the app. It contains no device identifier, no IP address, no location.

4.4 When you email us

If you email hello@duitful.app for support, feedback, or a bug report, the contents of your email, your email address, and any information you choose to include reach the operator directly. Inbound mail is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a personal inbox. We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the conversation and a reasonable period afterwards for reference; you can ask us to delete it at any time.

4.5 When you enable Pro's Google Drive sync

Pro includes an optional encrypted-backup feature that uses your own Google Drive as the storage destination. The feature is off by default; turning it on requires you to sign in with a Google account and grant Duitful permission to access only its hidden application data folder on Drive (the drive.appdata OAuth scope). Duitful cannot see, list, read, or modify any other file in your Drive — only the single backup file it writes for itself.

When sync runs, your device:

  • encrypts your records on-device with AES-GCM using your passcode-derived key (the same encryption used at rest), then
  • uploads that encrypted blob directly from your device to Google's Drive API, into the hidden app folder, as a single file.

Two consequences follow from this design:

  • Duitful runs no relay. The OAuth access token Google issues stays on your device, and your device talks to Google's servers directly. No request, no token, and no backup byte ever passes through any Duitful-operated server.
  • Google cannot read the backup. Google receives the file as an opaque encrypted blob. The passcode that decrypts it is never sent to Google or to us. To Google, the file is unreadable bytes.

What we record on your device when sync is enabled is listed in the local-storage keys table above (duit-tracker.drive). It does not include your passcode, your derived key, or any of your transaction data — only the OAuth token issued to your browser, the Drive file identifier, the Google account email, and the timestamp of the last sync.

You can disconnect Google Drive sync at any time from the in-app Cloud backup card. Disconnecting clears the OAuth token from your device and stops further uploads. The encrypted file already in your Drive is left where it is — you can delete it directly from Google's Drive > Settings > Manage apps screen, which also revokes Duitful's Drive permission.

Your interactions with Google through this feature are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms. Storage of the backup file counts against the Google Drive quota of the account you connect.

4.6 Website logs

The public website at duitful.app is served by GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub's servers receive standard HTTP request metadata — IP address, user-agent, referrer, requested path — which GitHub retains as part of its own operations under its own privacy policy. We don't operate our own web-access logging on top of that, and we don't receive or analyse those logs.

Our Pro-checkout endpoints are hosted on Vercel. When you hit those endpoints, Vercel retains similar request metadata for operational and security purposes, as described in Vercel's own privacy policy.

6. Android notification access

On Android only, Duitful can optionally use the Notification Listener permission to auto-capture bank and e-wallet transaction alerts. We take this permission seriously because it's one of the most sensitive a user can grant.

  • The listener is strictly opt-in. You choose to turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Android's system settings.
  • It only reads notifications from a hard-coded allow-list of Malaysian bank and e-wallet apps (Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank, RHB, Hong Leong, Touch 'n Go, Grab, Boost, BigPay, Shopee, Atome). Everything else is ignored.
  • Notification contents are parsed locally on the device. The parser extracts a candidate amount, merchant, and reference, and inserts a pending transaction into the app's on-device database for you to review.
  • No notification content is transmitted off the device. Duitful does not upload, forward, share, or back up notification text to any server, including our own.

7. Children

Duitful is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. How long we keep things

  • On-device data: as long as you keep the app installed. Uninstalling the app, clearing your browser's storage, or using the in-app "reset all data" option will delete it.
  • Encrypted Google Drive backup (Pro, optional): retained inside your own Google Drive until you delete it. Disconnecting Drive sync inside Duitful stops further uploads but leaves the existing file in place — remove it via Google's Drive > Settings > Manage apps screen.
  • Billplz payment records: retained by Billplz under its own policy.
  • Resend email logs: retained by Resend under its own policy (typically a rolling window measured in days to weeks for transactional mail).
  • Our Vercel function logs: retained by Vercel for up to roughly 30 days, then discarded.
  • Support email: kept for as long as is useful to help you, unless you ask us to delete it.

9. Your rights

You have rights over how your personal data is handled. The exact list depends on where you live, but Duitful applies the most protective regime everywhere — if your country's law gives you a stronger right, you get it.

9.1 If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the UK GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Art. 17) — ask us to delete personal data we hold, subject to limited legal exceptions.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18) — ask us to stop processing your data while a dispute is being resolved.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format. Inside Duitful, this is the in-app CSV export.
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing. Duitful runs no marketing automation, so in practice this is rarely needed.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making (Art. 22) — Duitful performs no automated decision-making or profiling, so this right is honoured by design.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) — withdraw any consent you previously gave, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal.
  • Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77) — complain to your national data protection authority. A directory is at edpb.europa.eu; UK residents can complain to the ICO.

9.6 How to exercise any of these rights

Email hello@duitful.app with the subject "Privacy request" and the right you want to exercise. We respond within 30 days (sooner where required by law). There is no fee for exercising your rights.

Because nearly all your data lives on your own device and is unreadable to us, most requests are answered by pointing you at the in-app CSV export, encrypted backup, or Reset all data tools — those give you the rights of access, portability, and erasure faster than we can.

10. International users and data transfers

Duitful is built in Malaysia, but the app works anywhere and some of our vendors operate worldwide. If you use Duitful from outside Malaysia, the small amount of data that does leave your device (your email for a Pro purchase, and any mail you send us) is processed in the regions where our vendors operate — primarily the United States (Vercel, Resend, Cloudflare) and Malaysia (Billplz).

For users in the EU, EEA, or UK, transfers of personal data to countries outside those regions rely on appropriate safeguards under GDPR Chapter V. Specifically:

  • Transfers to US-based vendors (Vercel, Resend, Cloudflare, Google for Drive sync) are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the vendor is certified, or by Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914) where it is not. Each vendor publishes its own transfer mechanisms; the latest status is on their respective privacy pages.
  • Transfers to Malaysia (for Billplz payment processing) are made with your consent or as necessary for the performance of the Pro purchase contract — recognised grounds under Art. 49 GDPR.
  • The encrypted Google Drive backup (Pro, optional) is end-to-end encrypted before it ever leaves your device, so even where the file is stored, the contents remain inaccessible to anyone without your passcode.

By using the service, you consent to these transfers where consent is the lawful basis. You can withdraw consent and stop further transfers at any time by deleting the app and emailing us to request removal of any remaining records (see Section 9).

11. Security

No system is perfectly secure, but the design of Duitful is intentionally pessimistic. Your financial data is encrypted on-device with AES-GCM before it is ever written to storage, using a key derived from your passcode through 250,000 PBKDF2 iterations. Because the key never leaves your device, a theoretical breach of any Duitful-operated system would not expose your records.

If we ever discover a security issue that affects users, we will post details on this website and notify affected users by email where possible.
