                    Reminders

                    A quiet notification that stays put, so you can type an amount without opening Duitful. It's filed for review next time you open the app — nothing is added on its own.

                    Money owed to you shows up here once it's past its due date — or, with no due date, after two weeks. Turn it off and only due dates you set will remind you.

                    Shows upcoming debt/bill due dates and pay days on Home. Browser notifications fire when the app is open and once per day for items due today. Background reminders (when the app is fully closed) need a native wrapper — not available on iOS Safari PWAs.

                    Security

                    Your data is encrypted with your passcode using AES-GCM (key derived from the passcode via PBKDF2). If you lose the passcode, the data is unrecoverable — export CSV regularly as a backup.

                    Your passcode is kept in this phone's hardware keystore and released only after a successful scan. It turns itself off if the phone's fingerprints or faces change; your passcode always works.
