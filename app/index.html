                CSV backup

                Your data lives in this browser (localStorage). Export to CSV to back it up or move to another device; import to restore.

                Cloud backup

                Sync your encrypted backup to your own Google Drive (hidden app folder). Your passcode encrypts the data before upload — Duitful and Google never see it unencrypted.

                Cloud backup isn't configured for this build. Set DRIVE_CONFIG.webClientId in app/drive-config.js.

                You're not Pro on this device. If you have a Pro backup in Google Drive, click Restore from cloud to bring it back — Pro will turn on automatically.

                Preferences

                Saved values stay the same — only the display currency changes.

                Currency rates

                Live mid-market rates for converting foreign-currency entries. Refreshed daily via the open-source Currency-API by @fawazahmed0 (public-domain, no API key, daily updates).

                Rates not loaded yet.

                Some currencies are display-only — live rates are not available for these.

                Reminders

                Shows upcoming debt/bill due dates and pay days on Home. Browser notifications fire when the app is open and once per day for items due today. Background reminders (when the app is fully closed) need a native wrapper — not available on iOS Safari PWAs.

                Security

                Your data is encrypted with your passcode using AES-GCM (key derived from the passcode via PBKDF2). If you lose the passcode, the data is unrecoverable — export CSV regularly as a backup.
