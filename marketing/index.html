02 · The avalanche method

Debt payoff · Links to crush-credit-card-debt-avalanche-2026

The fastest legal way to kill credit card debt: 1. List every debt with its APR. 2. Pay minimums on all. 3. Throw every spare ringgit at the HIGHEST APR one. 4. When it dies, roll that payment into the next highest. That's the avalanche method. Duitful does the maths for you. Full walkthrough → duitful.app/guides/crush-credit-card-debt-avalanche-2026
Cara paling cepat (& halal) habiskan hutang kad kredit: 1. Senaraikan semua hutang dengan APR masing-masing. 2. Bayar minimum untuk semua. 3. Lebihan duit, baling ke hutang APR PALING TINGGI. 4. Bila settle, gulingkan bayaran tu ke hutang seterusnya. Itu method avalanche. Duitful kira untuk kau. Penuh penerangan → duitful.app/guides/crush-credit-card-debt-avalanche-2026
RM 12,000 in credit card debt at 18% APR. Paying minimums only? You'll be in debt for 30+ years and pay almost RM 25,000 in interest. Add RM 500 extra a month to the highest-APR card first (the avalanche method) and you're debt-free in ~26 months — saving over RM 18,000 in interest. Duitful runs that simulation the moment you add a debt. No spreadsheet, no guesswork. Just your real timeline.
