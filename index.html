Why avalanche

Pay the expensive debt first. The math is ruthless, but it's right.

Snowball feels good because you clear small balances fast. But interest is interest: a credit card at 18% is burning more money every month than a personal loan at 7%.

Avalanche aims every extra ringgit at the highest-APR debt. You'll pay less interest overall, and you'll be free sooner. Duitful sorts it automatically, shows you the gap, and re-plans instantly when life changes.

Questions

The short answers.

Why one-time pricing and not a subscription?

A tracker shouldn't cost more than the problem it solves. Pro is a one-time RM 19.90. You keep the app forever, get every future Pro feature, and never see a renewal email. Server costs are near-zero because your data stays on your phone, so a subscription would just be charging you to subsidise someone else's growth metrics.

Does my data really never leave the phone?

Correct. All your transactions, debts, and goals live in an AES-GCM-encrypted database on your device, locked with a PBKDF2-derived key from your passcode. There is no sign-up, no sync server, no analytics SDK on your financial data. Receipt OCR happens via a privacy-focused API only when you explicitly scan, and no receipts are stored remotely.

Which banks are supported for auto-capture?

On Android, Duitful parses notifications from major Malaysian banks and e-wallets: Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank, RHB, Hong Leong, HSBC, TNG eWallet, GrabPay, Boost, Shopee Pay, and more. You review each transaction before it's saved. iOS doesn't allow notification reading, so on iPhone you'll use receipt scans and manual entry instead.

Can I use Duitful outside Malaysia?

Yes. The app supports MYR, SGD, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, THB, IDR and more, with automatic FX conversion for travel expenses. Singapore users in particular get full BNPL coverage (Atome, SPayLater, ShopBack PayLater). Auto-capture notification parsing is currently tuned for Malaysian banks, with Singapore banks next on the roadmap.

What happens if I lose my passcode?

Your data cannot be recovered. This is the trade-off for real end-to-end privacy. Because Duitful never has a copy of your passcode or your data, I can't reset it for you. I strongly recommend writing your passcode down somewhere safe, or using Pro's encrypted-backup export so you can restore to a new device.

Can I import from Monarch, YNAB or a CSV?

Yes. Duitful Pro imports CSV files in the standard formats exported by Monarch, YNAB, Money Lover, and most Malaysian bank statements. You can map columns manually if your export is non-standard. Export works the same way, and your data is yours to take anywhere, any time.

Was Duitful built with AI?

Yes. Most of the code, this landing page, and the guides were written with help from Claude Code (an AI coding assistant). The product idea, the original Excel sheet, and what gets shipped are mine. I review every change before it goes out. The full source is on GitHub if you want to check anything.

How does Pro sync across devices?

Pro syncs through your own Google Drive, not a Duitful server. Your encrypted database is written to a single file in your Drive. Other devices sign in with the same Google account, pull the file, and decrypt it locally with your passcode. Duitful runs no sync server. The passcode never leaves your device, so the data sitting in Drive is unreadable to anyone else, including Google and including me.
