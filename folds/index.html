Duitful on folding phones

Pick a phone, fold it, and see where everything goes. These are the layouts planned for iPhone Duo, Galaxy Z Fold and Flip; the app doesn't do this yet.

The rules follow Apple's iPhone Duo guidance: one layout that grows into the space, the tab bar on the side where the screen is short, list and detail side by side when there's room, and nothing you tap on the fold. A half-open fold splits the screen in two; flat, it's one screen.

Sizes are approximate. Apple hasn't published iPhone Duo's point sizes and the Samsung figures are converted from pixels, so treat them as a sketch until they're measured in the simulators. The same file drives this page and Duitful's tests: fold-lab.js.
